## Commonwealth Games 2022 & 2026: Nations, Medals & Events Dashboard

This dashboard explores how the Commonwealth Games evolved between the 2022 (Birmingham) and 2026 (Glasgow) editions, focusing on three angles: participating nations, medal performance, and changes in the event lineup.

## Data preparation:
Data was sourced and structured manually from Wikipedia (2022 & 2026 Commonwealth Games pages), applying data validation and correction methods to ensure accuracy. This included combining tables, adding derived columns, and cross-checking values against source pages to fix inconsistencies before building the dashboard in Tableau.

## Key features:

- Interactive map showing participating nations by year, with first-time participants and member country filters
- Medal tally comparison across ranked countries, broken down by gold/silver/bronze
- Event status tracking (Added / Continuing / Discontinued) across all sports and disciplines
- A dedicated India performance breakdown by sport, highlighting medals lost due to discontinued events
- Dashboard actions: highlighting a country on the map updates its corresponding row in the medal table

## India's Medal Performance
The dashboard also includes a dedicated breakdown of India's medal tally by  sport, distinguishing between sports still active and those discontinued after 2022. 
Discontinued sports(Hockey, Lawn bowls, Table Tennis, Badminton, Cricket, Squash and Wrestling) alone accounted for 32 medals — over half (52%) of India's 2022 tally. 
While overall medals dropped by 22 (a 36% decline) from 61 to 39.
However performance in continuing sports actually improved, gaining 10 medals and partially offsetting the loss from discontinued events.

## Tools used:

Compiled and cleaned by the author from Wikipedia (Commonwealth Games 2022 & 2026 pages).
